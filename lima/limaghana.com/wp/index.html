Accounting and Bookkeeping Services

We provide day-to-day bookkeeping and accounting services. We also cater for preparation of financial statements according to International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)

Payroll Services

We assist with maintaining the details of a company’s employees, calculating and making payments and deductions to the employees and the relevant authorities.

Consulting Services

Contact us for preparation of business plans, policies and procedures manuals, due diligence, mergers and acquisition, Internal audit and investigations

Tax Advisory & Compliance Services

We can support with providing interpretation to tax laws, giving regular updates on changes to tax laws, identifying risk and advising on mitigation strategies and tax planning, tax structuring for projects.

Regulatory Compliance Services

We do registration and renewal of Business operation permits, registrations, payments and filing of Pensions returns and obtaining SSNIT clearance certificates. We also support with the renewal of tax exemption approvals.
